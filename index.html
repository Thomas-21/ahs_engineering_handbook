Autonomous haulage systems

From truck behavior to mine production.

The AHS Engineering & Operations Handbook project is building an English-language handbook and a companion simulator for learning how autonomous haulage works in open-pit mines.

Explore the project Watch the demo

What we’re building

The handbook explains the engineering and operating decisions behind AHS. The simulator shows how those decisions play out across a fleet in a reference open-pit mine.

Engineering & Operations Handbook

Explore mine operations, systems architecture, vehicle autonomy, fleet dispatch, and deployment through a searchable outline of 23 volumes and 187 chapters.
