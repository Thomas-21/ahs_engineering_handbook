Appendix C — Reference mine

Reference Mine (fictional)

About this mine

Brownfield open-pit mine in a fictional Australian setting. One pit (North Pit), one primary crusher (C01) and one waste dump (D01). Mixed autonomous and manned equipment. Central control room at the mine infrastructure area (MIA).

Operating pattern: 24/7, two 12 h shifts [A]. Control room: CR1 at MIA (single central control room) [H]. Numerical values are illustrative engineering assumptions [A] unless tagged otherwise; the mine is not an actual mine and does not describe any vendor's system. Coordinates use a local grid (REFMINE-GRID), deliberately not a geodetic system, so the mine cannot be mistaken for a real place.

Single source: handbook/shared/reference-mine/mine.yaml (mine_version 0.3.0) and equipment.yaml. Segment lengths and grades are computed from node coordinates (ahs_sim).

What the reference mine leaves out

Every chapter uses one reference mine: a fictional brownfield open-pit mine in a fictional Australian setting, with one pit, one primary crusher (C01) and one waste dump (D01). It leaves out ore grade control and blending, more than one crusher or dump, other climates, the rules of other jurisdictions and any vendor's system. Lab results describe this mine only.
